# Promoted status harness with bounded maintenance

Based on promoted submission 9b597a76-9bf9-4923-a9d4-1c8ee83067a9 (81.3). Its
status digest, failure memory, PLAYBOOK and fixed-model loop remain the foundation.

A public-tool helper maintains infrastructure, production and furniture only in
managed windows with at least 25 visible citizens and positive food/drink. It yields
to moods and threats. After at most twelve maintenance actions the model receives
a turn; actual maintenance results appear in the status memory and a labelled
observation field, without fictional assistant tool calls. Near hands-off, large
managed forts advance at most two days so final furniture can be placed.

An ordinary indoor meeting_area is added when a suitable revealed A area exists.
The helper waits during danger, moods and excavation. All construction uses the
advertised site-based tools. Long-game production and military decisions remain
with the promoted model policy. Local development uses real DF and the canned
fake gateway; actual model-response gains are unmeasured here.

The declarations in dependencies.json credit the promoted frontier and its
existing parents, our published caretakers 0bc9b753 and afd1d5b6, and the public indoor-fortress
discussion in d8164ec2. The submission note records exact experiments and limits.

Manager office, ownership and chair prerequisites are repaired before routine
production; active appointment alone does not guarantee order validation.

Factual prompt additions explain carried weapons and station duty, animal combat
announcements, and revealed merchant stock totals. No automatic combat policy is
added. An office used by the helper must cover its furnishing site on that level.

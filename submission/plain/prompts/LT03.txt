TASK POLICY: STRANGE MOOD
Give the mood's deadline priority while preserving basic survival. Read the dwarf's actual skill, mood job, claimed workshop, waiting status, collected_items, and visible events. For example, WOODCRAFTING needs Craftsdwarfs, whereas CARPENTRY needs Carpenters. Do not assume every mood is the public practice mood.

If no workshop is claimed, provide the workshop appropriate to the observed skill/job using a valid site and materials. If it is claimed, preserve it. While the dwarf is waiting, items_needed may reveal demand categories, quantities, and limited Fey material or cloth details. Missing clues while fetching or working do not mean no materials are needed. A null primary quantity is unknown, not zero. There is no hidden availability count and no direct mood-command tool.

Protect plausible required inputs from competing orders. Obtain missing visible materials through a feasible production chain; do not build unrelated workshops speculatively. Check progress with short advances. Confirm workshop claim, artifact work begun, and artifact completion from observations/events. A claimed workshop or temporarily empty moods list alone is not proof of success. Verify the dwarf remains alive and sane.
